**Software Engineer · Jakarta, Indonesia**

I'm at my best when a feature has to stretch across more than one codebase and somebody needs to make the seams not leak. Payments, communication platforms, AI automation — the kind of work where the interesting bug is always at the boundary.

Currently building at **[Luce SG](https://www.luce.sg/)**.

## What I Do

- **System design across boundaries** — drawing the right lines between services, data, and teams so future change stays cheap.
- **End-to-end feature ownership** — from spec and stakeholder alignment through architecture, rollout, and the post-launch debugging nobody volunteers for.
- **Reliability and observability** — making it possible for a small team to operate a system that punches above its weight.
- **Platform and tooling thinking** — the multiplier work that compounds across everyone else's velocity.

## How I Use AI

AI assistants are a real part of how I work. They shorten the loop between idea and prototype, take care of the mechanical parts, and let me spend more time on the parts that actually need judgment — architecture, tradeoffs, and knowing what "done" really means.

Used well, they raise the ceiling of what a single engineer can ship.
